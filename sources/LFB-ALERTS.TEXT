11 note alert
LisaFuji Browser 1.1 beta   (October 2026)^L^L
A text web browser for the Lisa.  Pages come from a LisaWeb gateway
through an RS-232 FujiNet on Serial B: the gateway fetches the page,
keeps the words and the links, and sends 14 lines at a time.  A picture
with a description is a link too: a small icon of it appears on the
page after the text; click the icon, or follow the link, for the big one.^L^L
Press H for the start page, G for an address, S to search, ? for help.
Click a link's number, or type it and press RETURN.^L
Tool 419314.  T. O'Connor (bmwcyclist), 2026.
